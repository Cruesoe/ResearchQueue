[b]Research never stops: every project you can start is queued automatically, and the next one starts the moment the last one finishes.[/b]

Research becomes opt-out instead of opt-in: rather than picking each project you want, you snooze the ones you don't. The Research tab becomes the queue, and your researchers keep working whether or not you open it.

I made this with [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3729878405]Node Research[/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585]The Progression Modpack[/url] in mind. Feedback is welcome.

[b]I use this mod to test research enhancements, so changes are likely.[/b]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Every project you can start joins the queue automatically, earliest era first and cheapest first within each era, and new research is added as soon as it becomes available.
[*]Drag cards to change priorities, use Move to top to send a project straight to the front, or Research now to switch to it at once; the project it replaces keeps its progress and goes back to the top of the queue.
[*]Snooze projects you don't want this playthrough; anything that depends on them is set aside too until you restore them.
[*]Before a snooze, a confirmation lists every project it would block.
[*]Each card shows its cost, how long it takes on its own and when it should finish, measured from your colony's actual research speed.
[*]A timings panel shows your research speed, the time left on the current project, when the whole queue will be finished and how long all remaining research would take.
[*]Blocked projects keep their place in the queue and say what they are waiting for.
[*]The details panel shows a project's description, what it unlocks and what it leads to, with the other prerequisites each of those still needs.
[*]Search filters the queue and the snoozed list by name or by anything a project unlocks.
[*]Buttons beside the settings switch to the vanilla research tree, or Node Research's tree when it is installed, for browsing, with a button there to come back; the queue keeps choosing research.
[*]Standard research, each Anomaly knowledge category and Gravtech have separate queues, since each has its own research slot.
[*]The research complete pop-up is replaced by a message, so fast research doesn't keep stopping the game. Turn the pop-up back on in the settings.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3729878405]Node Research[/url]: compatible. Its era, foundation and emergence rules are followed, foundation and emergence projects are tagged on their cards, its "Hide research from future tech levels" setting hides those projects here too, and its research queue is taken over when you add this mod. Its tree no longer opens after every finished project, since the queue starts the next one itself.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3609835606]Vanilla Gravship Expanded - Chapter 1[/url]: compatible. Gravtech research gets its own queue.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3778002533]Semi Random Research:P-Fork Continued[/url]: not compatible, because it also chooses what to research.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3715276023]Research: Auto[/url]: not compatible, because it also chooses what to research.
[*]Safe to add to an existing save.
[/list]
